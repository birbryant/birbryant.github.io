Two foraging monkeys: tree attraction, Goldilocks spacing, and sharing

Each monkey is pulled toward nearby unchecked trees and prefers to stay in a band of distances from its partner. Facts about checked trees are shared within range, with distance-dependent loss; a food report sends the partner straight to the tree.
